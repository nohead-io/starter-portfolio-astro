## This is a demo

This site is the **[Nohead portfolio starter](https://github.com/nohead-io/starter-portfolio-astro)**: a complete Astro site, a portfolio and a journal, whose content (projects, posts, people, categories and these pages) lives in a Nohead project and is read at build time with [Nohead's Astro loader](https://docs.nohead.io/adapters/astro). Copy it, connect it to your own project, and replace Long Section with your own story. Its source and setup instructions are [on GitHub](https://github.com/nohead-io/starter-portfolio-astro).

## Type

Headlines are set in **Inter Tight**, by Rasmus Andersson; the text, the interface and the logo in **Source Sans 3**, by Paul D. Hunt for Adobe. Both are released under the SIL Open Font License. The logo is set in Source Sans's true small capitals, like the lettering in a drawing's title block.

## How it works

- **Content** lives in [Nohead](https://nohead.io): a [collection](https://docs.nohead.io/guides/content-modelling) each for projects, posts, people, categories and pages. A project's gallery is one field holding a list of images, each with its alt text. Editors write and [publish](https://docs.nohead.io/guides/publishing) in Nohead's editor; drafts never reach this site.
- **Pages** are prerendered by Astro when the site builds, so every page is a plain file served from a CDN.
- **Publishing** sends a [webhook](https://docs.nohead.io/guides/webhooks) that rebuilds the site, so a published post is live about a minute later.
- **Images** are uploaded once, as originals, and every size the design needs comes from [the image's address](https://docs.nohead.io/guides/images), with nothing exported by hand.
- **Search** runs in your browser, straight against [Nohead's search](https://docs.nohead.io/guides/public-search) with a search-only token that sees published posts and nothing else.

## Colour

Black ink on white paper, and greys in between; no other colour. Photographs are shown in black and white, and a linked one lightens when you point at it. In the dark, the paper goes black and the ink goes white.
