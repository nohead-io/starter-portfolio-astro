Støb (Danish for "cast") is a two-person studio in Copenhagen that casts vases, bowls and stools in concrete, by hand, in moulds that change a little every time. About thirty pieces a week, no two alike. It worked in a basement and sold from a market stall, and it took a ground-floor shop on a quiet street to do both in one place.

## What we did

- **Design.** The casting floor at the back, behind a glass wall, so the shop sees the work being made. A floor drain and a hose, because concrete is wet until it isn't.
- **Interior.** Oak drying racks along the long wall, slatted so air moves through, sized for the moulds and labelled by the week. And the window: empty six days a week. On Thursdays at ten the week's pieces go in it and on sale, and the window empties again as they sell.

## What changed

People queue on Thursdays. On the other days they stop at the glass wall and watch the casting, which the studio didn't plan for and now opens the door for.
